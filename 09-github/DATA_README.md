# Input and output data

This dictionary describes the complete reproducibility package. The five files
under `data/` are supplied in the Zenodo archive, not in this GitHub copy. See
[README.md](README.md#data-and-complete-reproducibility-package) for the download
status and setup instructions. The CSV outputs are included here; the MAT outputs
are regenerated by the analysis.

Missing numeric observations are NaN. Sea-level and model matrices have time in
rows and stations in columns. Gridded MATLAB arrays have longitude × latitude ×
time order. Longitudes are degrees east, with western longitudes negative.
Decimal monthly years are year + (month − 1)/12; they identify calendar months.

## Five input files

### `data/sea_level.mat`

- `M`: 384 × 131 centered monthly TG records, mm, before the package's seasonal
  cycle removal. The observations and existing gaps are retained.
- `t`: January 1993–December 2024 decimal monthly years.
- `N`, `L`: station names and [longitude, latitude], in coastal station order.
- `provider`, `provider_station_id`: source provider and station identifier.
- `river_mapping`: TG-to-river assignments used by the correction model.


### `data/altimetry_monthly.h5`

HDF5 datasets `/ALT`, `/lon` and `/lat` hold the regional monthly SLA+DAC grid in
mm and its spatial coordinates. Its 384 time slices follow `sea_level.mat`'s
`t` axis (January 1993–December 2024). The analysis reads chunks with `h5read`.
Seasonal cycles, ALT/TG correlations and offshore composites are recalculated.


### `data/forcing.mat`

- `U10`, `V10`: monthly zonal/meridional 10-m wind, m/s.
- `SLP`: monthly sea-level pressure, Pa.
- `lon`, `lat`, `tERA5`: atmospheric-grid coordinates and times.
- `FC_monthly`, `tFC`: Florida Current transport in Sv and its monthly times;
  1 Sv = 10^6 m³/s. The analysis selects 1993–2024.

### `data/river_monthly.mat`

- `months`: January 1993–December 2024 calendar months (384 rows).
- `Q`: 384 × 105 monthly individual-gauge means **after** 80% daily-coverage
  screening, in m³/s. Months below 80% coverage are stored as NaN. Retained means
  use the available finite daily measurements in that month.
- `valid_days`, `coverage`: contributing daily counts and counts/calendar days.
  Months without measurements have count/coverage zero and mean NaN.
- `members`: USGS site ID (retaining leading zeros), station name, coordinates,
  river membership, downstream-reference flag, and normalization start year.
- `NRD`, `LRD`, `reference_sites`: 31 river names, downstream-reference coordinates
  and site IDs; `tRD` is the final 1993–2024 time axis.
- `units`: `m3 s-1`.

The monthly records were averaged from USGS daily observations with at least 80% observations for each month.
Original cubic feet per second were converted using
0.028316846592 m³ per ft³. 
Daily records and acquisition code are not included in this package.

Multi-gauge indices remove each gauge's mean log10 flow, average available log anomalies by month,
restore the downstream reference's mean log10 flow, and exponentiate.
Single-gauge indices use the monthly discharge directly.

### `data/ancillary.mat`

- `gnss`: the corrected matched GNSS table for the 131 TGs; rates and uncertainties
  in mm/yr.
- `gnss_global_metrics`: source metrics used to calculate the quality-screening thresholds.
- `cape_may_gnss`: CMAP source observations, including Modified Julian Date and
  ellipsoidal height in m. The script converts to mm and derives monthly anomalies.
- `gia.mean`, `gia.std`: original ICE-7G mean/ensemble-SD grids; each contains
  `x`, `y`, `z`, with grid values in mm/yr. Station interpolation is recalculated.
- `bathy`: `lon_sub`, `lat_sub`, `Z_sub` for the regional bathymetry; elevations in m.
- `river_miles`: Delaware and St. Lawrence river-mile tables (US customary miles).
- `layout`: inset positions used for Figure 9 (plotting metadata).

## Model outputs

`outputs/results.mat` groups the results in six structures:

| Structure | Main contents |
|---|---|
| `R` | `MRD` river indices (384 × 31), names, coordinates, coverage and contributor counts |
| `O` | `M`, `L`, `N`, `t`, TG seasonal anomalies, selected offshore cells/weights, composite `ALT` and raw `ALT_TG` |
| `A` | ALT–TG, predicted near-coastal signal, corrected records, rates, HAC errors, counts and predictor summaries |
| `Models` | One model and predictor structure per TG: coefficients, covariance, selection, fit mask, predictor matrix, local wind PCA scores/loadings |
| `C` | Combined, wind-only and discharge-only fits for the main and supplementary analyses |
| `D` | GNSS quality/comparisons, GIA profiles, Cape May comparison, coherence, 10–28-year windows and trend adjustments |

Regression coefficient errors are conditional classical OLS errors and do not
include model-selection uncertainty. They are separate from HAC24 trend errors.
The local PCA bases in `Models` are needed to interpret or reuse wind-PC coefficients.

### Map coastline files

`external/m_map/data` contains `gshhs_l.b`, `gshhs_i.b` and `gshhs_h.b`
(low, intermediate and high resolution). These are unchanged copies of the
coastlines used to make the figures. Full-resolution coastlines, river and border
files, the original download ZIP and image examples are not needed and are omitted.
M_Map source notices and its HTML documentation are retained. The coastline
source README, `LICENSE.TXT` and `COPYING.LESSERv3` are retained beside the data.
These third-party notices apply to the bundled mapping files.

### Bathymetry and river-mile provenance

Bathymetry is from [NOAA ETOPO1: Topography and Bathymetry](https://sos.noaa.gov/catalog/datasets/etopo1-topography-and-bathymetry/),
the 1-arc-minute global relief model integrating land topography and ocean bathymetry.

The Delaware river-mile positions were assigned manually by William Coronel for
this study, with river mile zero at the river mouth and values assigned by counting
points upstream.
